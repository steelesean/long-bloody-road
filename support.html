Support

If something has gone wrong, or you have a question about the game, email CONTACT_EMAIL. It helps to say which iPhone or iPad you have and what you were doing when it happened.

I bought Hill 192 and Operation Cobra and they are locked again

Open the game's Settings with the gear on the first screen and tap Restore purchases. You will also find Restore purchases under the Buy button whenever one of the two missions is picked. Make sure the device is signed in to the same Apple Account that made the purchase. You are never charged twice for the same missions.

Where are my saved games?

On your device, inside the game. They are not stored online and do not move between devices by themselves. Deleting the game deletes them.

There is no sound

The game follows your device's silent switch. Turn silent mode off, turn the volume up, and check that Sound is on in the game's Settings.

I would like a refund

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.
