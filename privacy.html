Long Bloody Road

Privacy

Last updated 13 November 2026

Long Bloody Road is a WW2 tactics game for iPhone and iPad. This page says what the game does with your information. The short answer is that it collects none.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts and no sign-in. It shows no adverts. It has no analytics and no tracking, and it sends nothing about you or how you play to us or to anyone else.

Your saved games

Your campaign, your men and your settings are saved on your own device, in the game's own storage. They are never sent to us. Deleting the game deletes them. They may be included in your own device backups, which are run by you and Apple.

Purchases

Hill 192 and Operation Cobra are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment. We never see your name, your card or your Apple Account. The game is told only whether the two missions are unlocked. Apple's own privacy policy covers the purchase: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children

The game collects no information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this page

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated before the change is released, with a new date at the top.
